About the Author

Ihor Kendiukhov

University of Tübingen · Department of Computer Science

Ihor Kendiukhov is a bioinformatician and machine learning and quant finance professional. His primary research interests include deep learning in systems biology, econophysics, and multiagent artificial intelligence.

Additional interests include AI safety, growth economics, decision theory, longevity, and neuroscience.

His initial background is in quant finance and venture capital, but he focuses on DeepTech in ML, bioinformatics, and neuroscience. He gravitates toward generalist roles in startup environments where technology and business intersect, increasingly drawn to artificial intelligence, data science, systems architecture, and automation.

The work of Ole Peters and his colleagues in ergodicity economics fascinated Kendiukhov for a long time, and several years ago, while being a student in economics, he did his first programming experiments on the topic. He created the Ergodicity Library — a Python toolkit designed to automate and accelerate research in ergodicity economics and related fields.
