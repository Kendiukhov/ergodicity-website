How to Participate in the Development of the Ergodicity Library

In the near future, I expect to work on the following aspects and components of the library:

  • Ensuring that the agents module works fully, especially the parts that concern neural networks, evolutionary algorithms, and machine learning, while dealing with the inherently noisy nature of stochastic training data.
  • Speed optimization and integration with C and Wolfram Language to increase computation speed and improve symbolic computations where SymPy may not cope.
  • Integration with domain libraries, primarily in economics, finance, and biology, including import and export workflows and domain-specific classes.
  • Deeper implementation of multi-core computations and parallelism so that tasks related to modelling stochastic processes can be solved more efficiently.
  • Implementation of additional functionality based on articles and research on ergodicity economics not included in Peters and Adamou (2018).
  • Bringing to a full-fledged state methods that do not yet work as expected, such as characteristic-function based process construction and stochastic partial differential equation tooling.
  • Exploring mathematical apparatus beyond Ito Calculus for stochastic and ergodic analysis.
  • Implementation of the library’s graphical interface, which will allow easy use of the library by anyone.
  • Additional functionality for fat-tailed processes, including improved simulation, symbolic computation, and pattern-finding tools.
  • Implementing feedback and wishes from library users and testers.

Any help in these and other directions is welcome. I would especially appreciate the following help:

  • Integration with libraries in domain areas and the addition of new domain functionality.
  • Implementation of advanced mathematical tools for stochastic analysis and ergodic theory.
  • Testing the library.
  • Work on the graphical interface.
