Ergodicity Library: An Introduction is a comprehensive guide to the Ergodicity Library, a powerful Python toolkit designed for researchers in various fields exploring the fascinating world of ergodicity economics and stochastic processes.

This book offers:

  • A thorough introduction to the library’s capabilities and structure.
  • Practical examples and case studies demonstrating real-world applications.
  • In-depth explanations of key concepts in the library.
  • Step-by-step tutorials for implementing complex simulations and analyses.
  • Insights into time-average behaviour in various stochastic environments.

Whether you are a seasoned researcher or a curious student, this book provides the tools and knowledge needed to delve into the cutting-edge field of ergodicity economics and beyond.
