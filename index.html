from ergodicity.process.basic import BrownianMotion

bm = BrownianMotion()
data = bm.simulate(
    t=10,
    timestep=0.1,
    num_instance=1000,
    plot=True
)
Capabilities

Everything you need for
stochastic analysis

From simulating Brownian motion to training AI agents under uncertainty — a complete research toolkit.

∿

Stochastic Processes

Simulate and visualize Brownian motion, Lévy processes, fractional processes, and custom stochastic dynamics with full parameter control.

∂

Symbolic SDE Solver

The most powerful symbolic stochastic differential equation solver developed in Python. Supports systems of SDEs and partial SDEs.

⟨⟩

Time & Ensemble Averages

Analyze ergodic properties, convergence behavior, and the divergence between time averages and ensemble expectations.

◈

Artificial Agents

Create and train AI agents for decision-making under uncertainty using evolutionary algorithms and neural networks.

⫘

Multi-core Computing

Parallel processing support for large-scale simulations. Run thousands of process instances across all available cores.

◐

Dynamic Visualization

3D interactive graphs, animated trajectories, and publication-ready plots with Matplotlib and custom rendering.
