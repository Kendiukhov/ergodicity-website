About

The Ergodicity Library is a Python-based open-source library focused on the study of stochastic processes, ergodicity, and time-average behaviours.

Initially inspired by the work of Ole Peters and Alexander Adamou in ergodicity economics, the library can be applied in fields such as biology, physics, finance, and more.

It provides tools for simulating and analyzing stochastic processes, with a particular focus on processes with heavy tails, and enables research on non-ergodic dynamics.
