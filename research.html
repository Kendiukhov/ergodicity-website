Ergodicity Economics February 2024 · arXiv:2402.03374

Risk Aversion in Non-Ergodic Systems

Demonstrates through simulation that when economic agents undergo evolutionary processes involving random variation and selection based on prediction accuracy, they develop risk-averse behavior. Agents assign lower probabilities to "good" events and higher probabilities to "bad" events. Subjective probabilities align with those derived by assuming agents prioritize time averages over ensemble averages. When agents focus on time averages, "the need for the concept of risk aversion disappears."

Macroeconomics February 2024 · arXiv:2402.01938

Present Value of the Future Consumer Goods Multiplier

Derives a formula for the present value of future consumer goods multiplier based on the assumption of a constant share of investment in consumer goods production. The present value follows an infinite geometric sequence pattern. Investigates how multiplier concepts apply to macroeconomic analysis of capital and investment dynamics, building a model consistent with classical frameworks.
