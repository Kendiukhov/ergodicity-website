Coming in 2025

Adventures in Ergodicity Economics and Nearby

A forthcoming book about ongoing research in ergodicity economics using the Ergodicity Library.

This work focuses on exploring the behavior of time averages across various stochastic processes and their ensembles, applying these insights to model human and agent behavior under uncertainty, and addressing long-standing paradoxes in classical expected utility theory.

It also aims to apply the toolkit of ergodicity economics beyond economics to other domains.

While not attempting to provide definitive answers to all questions in the field, the book seeks to illuminate important areas for further research and demonstrate the potential of ergodicity economics in solving real-world problems.

The release date and additional details will be announced on the Ergodicity Library website. Subscribers to the library newsletter will receive updates as they become available.
